      Backup

      Your list lives only on this device. Export a copy now and then, so you can restore it if the site's data is cleared, if you remove the Home Screen icon, or when you move to a new phone.

      On an iPhone the Home Screen app and the same page in Safari keep separate lists. Export from one and restore into the other to bring your books across.

      Sync

      With sync on, the list saves itself after every change and comes back on any device you open the link on. Setting it up is a one-off — see the README.

      From Goodreads

      On goodreads.com: My Books → Import and export (left sidebar, near the bottom) → Export Library. Save the file somewhere your phone can reach, then pick it here. Shelves, ratings and reviews come across; covers fill themselves in afterwards.
